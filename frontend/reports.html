About this prototype

CNG Network Intelligence is an advanced decision-support demo focused on station-level demand forecasting, sales & RTO-driven growth, and optimized network expansion planning for Gujarat CNG infrastructure. Layout and interaction patterns are inspired by the reference digital twin while adding live scenario controls, multi-model forecasts, risk overlays and transparent ranking logic. All numerical forecasts remain synthetic for illustration.
